# by_human/

Empty, and that is the point.

A value lands here when a person has looked at the scan and confirmed it. Until
then the numbers in `by_claude/` are a machine's reading of a page image —
better than OCR, and wrong in different ways, but not checked by anyone.

When a value is confirmed, copy its row here with who checked it and when.
`build/` prefers a `by_human/` value over the `by_claude/` one for the same
cell, so confirming something changes what gets built.
