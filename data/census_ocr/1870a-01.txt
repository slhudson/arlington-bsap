DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR, CENSUS-OFFICE,
Washington, D. C., August, 24, 1872.
SIr: I have the honor to report that the last of the tables to be comprised in the volume on the Population of
the United States at the Ninth Census, has been sent to press to-day.
The first table of this volume was sent to press June 20, 1871. On the 2d of September, 1871, 1,000 copies of
the first four tables were obtained on Department requisition and distributed to members of Congress and to the
newspaper press.
On the 26th of December, 1871, I had the honor to submit a report of the operation of the Census Bureau dur-
ing the year then expiring, which report will be embodied in the volume now in press, and will present substan-
tially all the remarks which are deemed necessary in the publications of the present Census, except such notes and
explanations as would be more appropriate in immediate connection with the individual tables to which they relate.
By concurrent resolution of March 8, 1872, 12,000 copies of the first eight tables were ordered printed; and by
concurrent resolution of May 23, 1872, another edition of 12,000 was authorized. The number of copies of the com-
plete volume authorized by the concurrent resolution of April 19, 1871, is 25,000. The maps which are to illustrate
this volume are already under contract and will be delivered to the Congressional Printer on or before the date at
which it is expected the edition will be ready for binding.
The latter half of this volume has been prepared for publication amid the duties and distractions of another
Bureau, to the charge of which I was assigned in November, 1871. I trust, however, that an examination of these
tables will not disclose any want of labor or of care in the superintendence of the Census work.
I have the honor to be, very respectfully, your obedient servant,
FRANCIS A. WALKER,
Superintendent.
Hon. O. DELANO,
Secretary of the Interior. .
