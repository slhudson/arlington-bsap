\part{Historical Background}

Following the Civil War, Virginia's constitution of 1869 created a board of
supervisors in every county, and that is where this report begins.\autocite[art.~VII,
sec.~2]{vaconstitution1869} Alexandria County, as Arlington was then called, elected
one supervisor from each of its three townships, Arlington, Jefferson and Washington,
in May 1870,\autocite[chs.~39, 76 and 188]{vaacts1870} and an 1875 act renamed the
townships magisterial districts, keeping their lines, names and voting places the
same (Figure~\ref{fig:residents-district-map}).\autocite{vaacts1875} This report
follows the land the Board governed at the time: the three districts, with the land
Alexandria annexed in 1915 and 1930 counted until it was annexed, and never the
City of Alexandria itself.
