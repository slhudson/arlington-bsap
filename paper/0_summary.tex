In 2026 the Arlington County Board sought an independent, non-partisan
examination of the structure and performance of the County's form of
government. Its scope of work\autocite{arlingtoncounty2026scopeofwork} asked
for three things: a historical analysis of how Arlington's form of government
developed and how that history bears on its performance and public
participation today; a synthesis of the community feedback the Board has
received on it and of how future engagement might proceed; and an outline of
the concepts and questions the Board might take up in any future work on its
structure and on public participation. The National Civic League leads the
work, with Sally Hudson and Alex Keena.

This report answers the charge in three parts, one for each deliverable. Part A
is the history of the Board: how it has been built, how its members have been
elected, who has served on it, and what Virginia law has let the County decide
for itself. Part B is what residents have said, in public comment, in letters to
the Board and in the County's surveys. Part C sets out the questions the first
two parts point toward.
