Following the Civil War, Virginia adopted a new state constitution in 1869,
which created a board of supervisors in every county, and that is where this
report begins.\autocite[art.~VII, sec.~2]{vaconstitution1869} The territory now
known as Arlington County was then called Alexandria
County.\autocite[note~2]{forstall1996}\fnsep\footnote{The Nacotchtank, whom the
English also called Necostins, lived on this shore of the Potomac when John
Smith charted the river in 1608; his map places a village, Nameroughquena, in
what is now Arlington, and by 1679 they had left it, to disease, war and the
loss of their fishing, hunting and farming grounds
(\cite{arlingtonva2011markers}; \cite{nps2025rooseveltisland}).}
